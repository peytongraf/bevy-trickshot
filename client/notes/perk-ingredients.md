# Perk ingredients

Perks are similar to cod perk a colas. They are drinks which give a perk and have a side effect.
The ingredients should be similar to cod perk a cola ingredients but mostly original. They should at least be somewhat related to what the perk does meaning these ingredients at least somewhat cause the effect the perk does even if the effect is greatly exageratted. Some of the ingredients can be really ridiculous and comical.

## Nitro Brew:

Pro - Increase ads, movement, weapon swap, rechamber, and reload speeds
Side Effect - Not decided yet

### Ingredients

- Caffeine Anhydrous
- Distilled rocket fuel
- Methamphetamine
- Dash of preworkout
- Crushed adderall
- Splash of nitroglycerin
- Twelve energy drinks, boiled down to one shot
- Freeze-dried hummingbird heartbeats
- Squirt of WD-40 for smoother reloads
- Cheetah sweat

## Shroom Tea

Pro - Player can see glows through walls where enemies are and they get aim assist
Side effect - Players vision begins to look like they are actually tripping on psilocybin mushrooms

### Ingredients

- Heroic dose of psilocybin mushrooms
- Fresh-squeezed lemon juice (lemon tek for a faster kick-in)
- Bioluminescent foxfire fungus (makes enemies glow)
- Lion's mane extract for laser focus
- Cordyceps, harvested straight off a zombie
- Carrot concentrate and bilberry for night vision
- Owl eyeball garnish
- Ground-up x-ray film
- Third eye drops
