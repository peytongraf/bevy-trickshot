# Intro

[low, gravelly voice; calm, authoritative military radio delivery] This is Actual. Listen carefully.

[slow, ominous] You're standing inside a structure that predates anything our intel boys can make sense of.

[tense, controlled] We've got movement inside the temple. No thermal signatures we can trust. No comms from the previous team. And something down there just answered our radio check.

[dry, darkly amused] Well, fuck me sideways. Command said this was a recovery operation. Didn't mention we'd be recovering a bunch of corpses... or adding our own to the fucking pile.
[ laughs softly ]

[uneasy, almost whispering] Whatever the hell they were doing here... we're about to find out.

# First dog round

[intense, afraid, fast speaking, growling, slow pause before the final sentence] "Actual to all units... command's picking up K9 signatures in the fog. No friendly units in the area... so whatever the fuck is growling out there... it sure as hell ain't on a leash."

# First boss round

[low, gravelly; tense military radio delivery] Actual to all units... thermals just picked up Big Steve. Staff Sergeant Steven Barrett, six-foot soldier before some half-assed vaccine trial turned him into a nine-foot, radioactive fucking nightmare. [dry, dark humor] Guess the side effects weren't listed on the goddamn label. [pause; voice drops to a near whisper] ...And he's heading straight for you.

# NOT RECORDED YET: ideas (not in game)

Everything below this line is suggestions only. Nothing here has a recording or is wired up yet.
Short calls (a few seconds each) that point the squad at what to do while keeping the mystery
going. When one gets recorded and wired up, move it above the divider.

## Power turned on (once per game)

[tense, then low] "Actual. Grid's live at your location. ...Strange. I'm reading more draw than lights alone could pull. Something down there was waiting for that switch."

## Power still off at the start of round 3 (only if it's off)

[dry, impatient] "Actual to all units. You're still fighting in the dark. There's a breaker somewhere on that site. Find it. Whatever's down there sees better in the dark than you do."

## First Pack-a-Punch (once)

[stunned, quiet] "...What the hell did that machine just do to your weapon? My readings went off the scale. Don't ask me how it works. Just use it."

## First Mystery Box spin (once)

[uneasy] "That box isn't on any manifest I've seen. Our people logged it as 'not from here.' Take what it gives you... and don't stand there staring at it."

## First door opened (once)

[low, cautious] "Actual. You just opened up a part of the site our drones never made it into. Keep your eyes open. Last team that went that deep stopped transmitting."

## Der Wunderfizz wakes up (round 17)

[slow, unsettled] "Something just powered on at your location. Nobody touched it. ...It turned itself on. Go see what it wants."

## First dog round cleared (once)

[quiet, thinking aloud] "K9 signatures are down. ...I pulled the thermal from that fog. They didn't come out of it. They came *through* it. From where, I don't know yet."

## Big Steve killed (first boss kill, once)

[relieved, then dark] "Confirm, Big Steve is down. Hell of a job. ...Barrett wasn't the only volunteer in that trial. Keep that in mind."

## Exfil becomes available (round 11)

[measured] "Actual. Got you a ride. There's a radio on site still tied into the relay. Call it and I'll send a bird. Fair warning: the second it spins up, every dead thing down there hears it."

## Exfil called

[fast, urgent] "Copy, bird's inbound. Clear that LZ. Kills outside it don't count for shit. Move!"

## Round 10

[grim] "Ten waves. Most teams don't see five. ...Command's stopped asking if you're alive. Now they're asking what you've *found*."

## Last player standing

[calm, close to the mic] "You're the last one up. Everyone's on you now. Breathe. Pick your shots. Get them back on their feet."

## Rampage Inducer turned on

[alarmed] "Did you just flip something? Every hostile on my thermal just picked up speed. ...I sure as shit hope you meant to do that."

## Game over (everyone down)

[long pause, quiet] "All units, come in. ...All units. ...Mark the time. Site is lost. Get me the next team."
